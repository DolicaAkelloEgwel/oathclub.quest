Infrequently Asked Questions

What is Oath Club?

Oath Club is...
  • A time capsule of final-day-of-EMF energy.
  • An accountability tool to help ADHD peeps commit to projects.
  • A tool to help autistic peeps to know who can be trusted when they say "Let's hang out sometime!"
  • An art project?
One of The Oath Warden's gym buddies says to not try to define it. Maybe Oath Club is whatever you want it to be.

How do I take part in Oath Club?

You seek out The Oath Warden who will take your oath on the the final day of EMF. You must write down what it is you intend to do in The Big Shiny Red Book. The Oath Warden will be in a fixed location (most likely the lounge) during my office hours. But if you can't make the office hours you can also ring Oath Club HQ, and we will try to arrange a meeting for you instead.

Can I make an oath without being physically present?

It is best if you're there, so that The Oath Warden can look you in the eyes and measure your soul. It's also better if you state your oath in front of an audience who will all judge you next time if you fail to deliver.

What happens if I make a false oath?

That is for Mithra and Horkos to decide. Nothing. But you'll see the others who have gotten their "special prize" and possibly feel a bit jealous of them.

What happens if I can't uphold my oath at the next EMF, but am able to do this at one of the following EMFs?

That is fine, and your dedication is very honourable in the eyes of Oath Club. You will still get your special prize. Perhaps it will even be special-er.

What is the "special prize"?

All Oath Club can say for now is that it may or may not augment your badge...

But I don't have a badge...

Oath Club haven't yet thought about what to do in this case.

Does my oath need to be techy?

Not at all. But it should be something you can present evidence of having done.

What happens if I uphold my oath, but I'm unable to make it to the next EMF?

Fingers crossed Oath Club will try to post your special prize to you.
