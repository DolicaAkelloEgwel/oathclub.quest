Infrequently Asked Questions

What is Oath Club?

How do I take part in Oath Club?

You seek out me, The Oath Warden, who will take your oath on the the final day of EMF. You must write down what it is you intend to do in my Big Shiny Red Book. I will be in a fixed location (most likely the lounge) during my office hours. But if you can't make the office hours you can also ring Oath Club HQ, and we will try to arrange a meeting for you to write your oath.

Can I make an oath without being physically present?

It is best if you're there, so that The Oath Warden can look you in the eyes. And so that you state your oath in front of an audience who will judge you next time if you fail to deliver.

What happens if I make a false oath?

Nothing. But you'll see the others who have gotten their "special prize" and possibly feel a bit jealous of them.

What is this "special prize"?

All I can say for now is that it may or may not augment your badge...

But I don't have a badge..

We haven't yet thought about what to do in this case.

Does my oath need to be techy?

Not at all. But it should be something you can present evidence of having done.

What happens if I complete my project/goal, but I'm unable to make it to the next EMF?

We will try to post your special prize to you.
