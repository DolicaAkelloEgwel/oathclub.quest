Infrequently Asked Questions

What is Oath Club?

You swear an oath about something you intend to accomplish by the next EMF and write it down in The Big Shiny Red Book. Then at that next EMF you present evidence of upholding your oath. If you can do this, then Oath Club will reward you with a special prize. Oath Club could be seen as...
  • A time capsule of final-day-of-EMF energy.
  • An accountability tool to help ADHD peeps commit to projects.
  • A tool to help autistic peeps determine who can and can't be trusted when people say things like "Let's hang out sometime!"
  • An art project?
One of The Oath Warden's gym buddies says to not try to categorise it. Maybe Oath Club is whatever you want it to be.

How do I take part in Oath Club?

You seek out The Oath Warden who will take your oath on the the final day of EMF. You must write down your oath in The Big Shiny Red Book. The Oath Warden will be in a fixed location (most likely the lounge) during the Oath Club office hours. But if you can't make the office hours then you can also ring Oath Club HQ, and The Oath Warden will try to arrange a meeting at a time that works better for you.

Can I make an oath without being physically present?

It is best if you're there, so that The Oath Warden can look you in the eyes and weigh your soul. It's also better if you state your oath in front of an audience who will all judge you next time if you fail to deliver.

What happens if I break my oath?

That is for Mithra and Horkos to decide. Nothing. But you'll see the others who have gotten their special prize and possibly feel a bit jealous of them.

What happens if I can't keep my oath in time for the next EMF, but am able to complete my goal before one of the following EMFs?

That is fine, and your dedication is very admirable in the eyes of Oath Club. You will still get your special prize. Perhaps it will even be special-er...?

What is the "special prize"?

All the Oath Club team can say for now is that it may or may not augment your badge...

But I don't have a badge...

The Oath Club team haven't yet thought about what to do in this case.

Does my oath need to be techy?

Not at all. But it should be something you can present evidence of having done.

Does my oath need to involve something (workshop, talk, project, etc) that I bring to the next EMF?

No. Oaths related to your outside-of-EMF life are welcome.

What happens if I uphold my oath, but I'm unable to make it to the next EMF?

Fingers crossed the Oath Club team will post your special prize to you.

Can I make multiple oaths in the same year?

No. We need you to lock in on one goal. So choose wisely.
